About

Profile

I’m Kumar Aman, Creative Director and founder of The Minimical. I work across brand identity, motion design, commercial film direction, digital campaigns and creative production.

With 6+ years of experience and 500+ projects, I combine strategic thinking with high impact visual execution, from the first idea through final delivery.

Philosophy

Design is more than aesthetics. It is a tool for telling stories, building connections and creating impact.

I care about clarity, emotional resonance and the details that make a visual system feel inevitable rather than decorative.
